Additional practical recommendations

Based on the page implementation, which already includes supplier KPIs, health scoring, executive summaries, recommendations, risks, opportunities, analytics, and searchable supplier tables, I would also recommend these structural changes:

Merge Procurement Intelligence Summary, Executive Decision Center, and AI Procurement Recommendations into one Executive Procurement Insights section.
Move Supplier Opportunity Center immediately after Supplier Performance Cards, because opportunities naturally follow supplier performance.
Place Supplier Risk Intelligence directly after Supplier Dependency Analysis, since dependency is a major procurement risk.
Keep the Supplier Intelligence Table as the final section with a fixed-height, scrollable table.
Use a sticky action bar at the top (or floating on scroll) displaying:
Overall Supplier Health
High-Risk Suppliers
Single Source Products
Procurement Spend
Current Executive Action

These refinements would reduce the page length by roughly 25–30%, improve executive decision-making, and make the dashboard feel like a production-ready procurement intelligence platform rather than a long analytical report, while preserving all of the existing business logic and analytics.
