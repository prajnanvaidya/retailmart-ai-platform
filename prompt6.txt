The Supplier Intelligence page should not duplicate Product Analytics or Inventory Intelligence. It should answer one core business question:

"Are our suppliers reliable, profitable, cost-effective, and operationally healthy?"

This page is intended for procurement managers, sourcing teams, supply chain planners, finance teams, operations managers, and executives. It should present supplier performance in a practical way using the available RetailMart dataset while being extensible for future Databricks Genie integration.

Maintain the same dark enterprise theme, typography, spacing, cards, navigation, and reusable components already used throughout the application. Reuse shared components wherever possible and keep calculations inside helper functions rather than inside the UI.

Development Guidelines
Reuse existing UI components for consistency.
Keep all business logic in helper functions.
Ensure every KPI, chart, recommendation, and scorecard derives from the same dataset so the dashboard presents a coherent business narrative.
Avoid placeholder features or metrics that cannot be calculated from the available RetailMart data.
Prioritize readability with concise text, meaningful visualizations, and logical grouping of sections.
Design the experience to resemble a modern procurement analytics platform used by enterprise retailers, emphasizing actionable insights over decorative elements.
