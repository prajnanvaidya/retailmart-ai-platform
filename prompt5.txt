Product Analytics Center

Create a completely new Product Analytics Center from scratch.

The page currently contains no implementation.

The objective is to create an enterprise-grade Product Analytics dashboard used by category managers, merchandising teams, inventory planners, supply chain analysts, pricing teams, and executives.

This page should not duplicate Inventory Intelligence or Sales Forecast. Instead, it should answer the question:

"How are our products performing, and what actions should we take?"

Maintain the same dark theme, layout, spacing, typography, reusable components, and navigation already established in the application. Reuse components from components/ wherever possible (header.py, cards.py, charts.py, alerts.py, tables.py) and keep business logic in utils/helpers.py or SQL helper files. The page must be ready for future Databricks Genie integration, so presentation and business logic must remain separated.

Development Guidelines
Reuse existing UI components.
Keep business logic in helper functions.
Avoid hardcoded values or duplicate calculations.
Ensure all KPIs, charts, and recommendations are derived from the same data so the page tells a consistent business story.
Optimize spacing and readability for an executive dashboard.
The final result should feel like a modern merchandising analytics platform used by retailers such as Amazon, Walmart, Costco, or Reliance Retail, while remaining practical and implementable using the current RetailMart dataset.
