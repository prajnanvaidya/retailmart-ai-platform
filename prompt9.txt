Create a professional enterprise-grade Streamlit page called "AI Search".

This page should function as a conversational business intelligence assistant for RetailMart.

The chatbot MUST use the Databricks Genie Space named:

Retail Sales and Inventory Analytics

This Genie Space has already been created and configured. Integrate the chatbot with this existing Genie Space instead of building a new AI model or custom RAG pipeline.

The objective is to allow executives and business users to ask questions in natural language across all business domains including Sales, Products, Customers, Inventory, Suppliers and Procurement.
